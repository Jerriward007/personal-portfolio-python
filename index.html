ABOUT ME
Aspiring Bioinformatician • Web Developer • Data Analyst • Molecular Biologist • Pathologist

Hi there! I'm Ogungbemi Jeremiah Kola, a Plant Biologist and aspiring Bioinformatician with a strong passion for data science, programming, and applied genomics. I hold a B.Sc. in Botany from the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta. I am deeply passionate and dedicated to my profession. My journey in Botany has equipped me with a robust understanding of plant sciences, emphasizing the importance of sustainable practices.

Throughout my academic pursuits, I have honed skills in Plant Physiology, Genetics, Virology, Molecular Biology, Bioinformatics, Plant Pathology, and research methodologies, cultivating a keen interest in further developing myself in bioinformatics and biotechnology. I approach challenges with a proactive mindset, seeking innovative solutions to contribute meaningfully to the field. My commitment to continuous learning drives me to stay abreast of the latest developments in botanical sciences.

Beyond academics, I possess strong interpersonal and communication skills, fostering effective collaboration within diverse teams. I am adept at translating complex biological concepts into accessible knowledge for various audiences.

Outside of my academic and professional interests, I enjoy experimenting with code during my leisure time. I’m also a lover of good food — nothing beats a well-prepared plate of jollof rice and chicken, especially when accompanied by a chilled bottle of Fruita juice!

In addition to my scientific pursuits, I am a creative at heart. I'm a fashion designer and the founder of a growing brand called Estatiq Wears, which will be launching soon. I specialize in designing and sewing both traditional and international styles, with a strong attention to detail and originality. Whether you're looking for professional wear or cultural elegance, I'm available at your service.

I am now actively seeking opportunities to apply my skills and knowledge in a professional setting — particularly in the fields of bioinformatics, biotechnology, or genomics — where I can contribute meaningfully, grow continuously, and make a lasting impact. Let's connect and explore how we can collaborate!
